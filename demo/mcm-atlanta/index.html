Questions

Straight answers.

How much is a Hemi cam install?

A Stage 1 cam and lifter package for the 5.7 and 6.4 Hemi is $3,500. Stage 2 is $5,555. Stage 3, with headers and a stall converter, is $11,111. The 2015–24 Chevy LT1 Stage 1 cam install is $4,300.

Can you fix the Hemi tick?

A Hemi tick is often a failing lifter. We check it first and tell you what we find. Many owners do a cam and lifter package while the engine is already apart.

Do you tune after a cam?

Yes. A PCM unlock is $300, so your car can run the tune that matches your new parts.

Can you check a car before I buy it?

Yes. A pre-purchase inspection is $150. Know what you’re buying before you pay.

Do you come to me?

For some jobs, yes. Mobile service starts with a $50 trip fee. Ask when you book.
