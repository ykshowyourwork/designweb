Our food

Antojitos, the way they make them in el DF.

Antojitos are Mexico City's street food: masa pressed by hand, then filled, griddled and topped. Gorditas, huaraches, tlacoyos and pambazos, plus the chilaquiles people order more than anything else.

The sign on Pioneer Blvd says it: estilo DF
