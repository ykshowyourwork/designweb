Questions

Straight answers.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the size of the tree, how close it is to your house or power lines, and whether you want the stump ground. Most homeowners get a fair, free estimate from us the same day. Many say our price was lower than other quotes.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry insurance and can send you a copy before we start.

Do you clean up after?

Always. We rake, blow, and haul away the brush and wood, or stack it where you ask.

How do I pay?

Cash, card or check. Cards have a 3% fee. Ask about paying in installments.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Military and 55+ discounts.
