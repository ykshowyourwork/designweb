The details

What each part means.

Top of Google (SEO)

Each service and each town you work in gets its own page, with the words people actually search. Google can read every page, and your Google Business Profile points to your site.

Named by AI (AEO)

Hidden code on every page (called Schema) tells ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers your business name, services, area, hours and reviews, so they can recommend you.

Leads to your phone

Quote and booking forms text and email you right away. A hidden spam check keeps fake submissions out.

Payments

Customers pay deposits and invoices by card through Stripe. Receipts go out on their own.

Installs like an app

Customers can add your site to their home screen with your logo. It opens full screen, like an app. A listing in the App Store and Google Play is a $1,000 add-on.

Fast on phones

Most of your customers search on their phone. Every page is built for phones first and loads fast.
