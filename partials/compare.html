The difference

Having a website is like having money in the bank.

The real question is what that money is doing for you. Most websites just sit there. Yours should be working.

A website that sits there

  • Nobody finds it unless they already know your name
  • ChatGPT and Google's AI answers skip right past you
  • Visitors have to call to get a price or a time
  • You can't tell if it ever brought you a job

A website that works for you

  • Shows up at the top of Google for "[your service] near me"
  • Gets named when people ask ChatGPT who to hire
  • Quote requests and bookings land straight in your phone
  • New articles keep sending leads to your inbox, every month
