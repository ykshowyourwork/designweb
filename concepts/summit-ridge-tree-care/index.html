Good questions

Before you call.

Short answers to what people ask us most.

How much does tree removal cost?

It depends on the tree size and what is under it. Send a photo and we will give you a free written price, usually the same day.

Will insurance pay for my fallen tree?

If a tree hits your house, garage or fence, most home policies help pay to remove it. We take the photos and notes your insurance company asks for.

Do you grind the stump too?

Yes. We grind it below the grass line and clean up the chips, or leave them for mulch if you want.

Can you work near power lines?

We trim and remove trees near house lines every week. For the main lines on poles, we work with the power company to make it safe first.

How fast can you come after a storm?

Tree on a house or car comes first, day or night. We call you back within minutes and come as fast as it is safe.
