How it works

From first call to live site in about two weeks.

You see your homepage before you pay anything. Then it's half to start, half on launch day.

Step by step

What happens, and when.

Day 1 to 2: your free mockup

We design your homepage with your logo, your photos from Google and your best reviews. We walk through it together on a 15-minute call.

Day 2: deposit

You get an emailed invoice for half and pay by card. Nothing is due until you like the mockup.

Day 3 to 12: build

We build every page, write the words, set up your quote forms and connect your domain. You get a preview link to check along the way.

Day 13 to 14: launch

We go live, set up Google, and you pay the second half. Google usually starts showing new pages within a few weeks.

What we need from you

  • Your logo, if you have one
  • Photos of your work, or permission to use the ones on your Google listing
  • Access to your domain and Google Business Profile, or we set them up for you
  • 15 minutes for the mockup call
